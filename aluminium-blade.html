Aluminium Blade

Aluminium Saw Blade

Engineered for cutting non-ferrous metals and plastics. The specialized tooth geometry prevents material binding and ensures burr-free cuts on aluminium extrusions and profiles.

Key Features & Benefits

  • Specialized Cut: Negative hook angle prevents grabbing of material
  • Finish: Provides a burr-free, mirror-like finish on cuts
  • Material: Optimized carbide tips for cutting non-ferrous metals
  • Application: Ideal for aluminium window/door frames, extrusions, and plastics
  • Safety: Engineered to eliminate kickback during cutting
